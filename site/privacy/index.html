Privacy

Hotkey Runbook is local-first. The desktop app does not send your runbooks, parameters, command output, trusted-folder records, or execution history to us.

What stays on your device

Trusted directory signatures, a random device signing key, redacted execution history, theme preference, and any pasted license token are stored locally. Secret parameters are used for the requested process and redacted before history is written.

Network requests

Local runbooks do not need a network connection. The app contacts the Sociobot billing API only when you add or revalidate a paid license. The website requests GitHub release metadata to choose a download. We ship no analytics, ad pixels, third-party fonts, or runtime scripts.

Purchases

When new sales are enabled, Sociobot/Dodo processes checkout and refunds as merchant of record. Hotkey Runbook receives only the license verification result needed to enable paid features.

Your control

Remove trusted folders in Settings, clear history in the History view, remove the local license in Settings, or uninstall the app and delete its app-data directory. For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
